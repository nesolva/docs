# T-03: Ailita for organization administrators

- **Roadmap:** ../ROADMAP.md · **Phase:** 2 · **Repo:** nesolva/docs · **Base branch:** release/public-docs-p2 (PR targets it, not main) · **Branch:** docs/ailita-admin
- **Depends on:** T-01 merged · **Model:** sonnet

## Goal
An organization administrator can do the following in the Backoffice without contacting Nesolva:
- invite and manage users;
- create roles;
- choose how people sign in;
- verify a domain and set up corporate SSO;
- connect the billing mailbox for Alfred;
- handle access recovery requests;
- fix the common errors.

## Scope
- Files/areas: `es/ailita/admin/*.mdx`, eight stubs from T-01.
- Source: SOURCE-FACTS.md, section B, including its messages table, which goes in `admin/problemas`.
- Out of scope: `docs.json` and the end-user pages.

## Contract
- Roadmap Decisions and Contracts.
- `admin/introduccion`: what the Backoffice is, that it requires a second factor, and "Confirmá que sos vos para
  hacer cambios" before changes.
- `admin/roles`: base, product and organization roles, the Organization administrator and the Alfred Operator
  roles, and the rule that you can only grant permissions you have. Don't say that ADMIN and USER are equivalent to
  anything (unclear in the source).
- `admin/acceso-y-seguridad`: what each option means for the members. Don't recommend specific values for
  durations.
- `admin/dominios-y-sso`: domain, then TXT record, then verify, then SSO (Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace, Nesolva's
  app or your own app).
- `admin/casilla-de-facturacion`: sign in with the mailbox account, accept every permission, and reconnect when it
  disconnects.
- `admin/recuperacion-de-acceso`: always confirm identity through another channel before approving. Present it as a
  best practice for the organization, not as an explanation of how Ailita protects itself.
- Integrations: one paragraph, either in `admin/introduccion` or `admin/acceso-y-seguridad`, saying they are set up
  with Nesolva and that a new secret is shown only once. No technical details.
- `admin/problemas`: every message in table B, in the roadmap's troubleshooting shape.

### Notes for the planner
- **The content check blocks ordinary Spanish.** `check-public-content.sh` uses `grep -i` with `\b`, which treats accented letters as non-word characters. It therefore flags `todo` (the `\bTODO\b` pattern), `pendiente` (the `\bPENDIENTE\b` pattern), and `bot` inside `botón` and `método`. I worded around all four. Consequence: the recovery status **Pendiente** is written as "Pendientes" (plural). Suggest making the patterns case-sensitive and ASCII-bounded.
- **ES labels not in SOURCE-FACTS (described in words, not bolded or quoted):** the cancel action on invitations, the session settings (maximum length, idle timeout, how often to ask for 2FA, remember device, maximum concurrent sessions), "Create users automatically" and "Enabled" in SSO, the confirmation messages ("Changes saved…", "Mailbox connected…", "User disabled…").
- **Inferred, worth a check:** with "create users automatically" off, people need an invitation to use SSO (inferred from the Ailita ID message "Tu cuenta del proveedor no tiene acceso a esta organización"). **Bloqueado** is described only as "the sign-in is blocked", with a pointer to support, because SOURCE-FACTS says nothing about how to unblock.
- **Left out on purpose:** ADMIN and USER badges (unclear in the source), anything about durations and limits, and how the TXT record looks.
- **Integrations** paragraph is in `admin/introduccion`.
