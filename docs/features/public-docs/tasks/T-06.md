# T-06: Help: home, support, FAQ, changelog, data and privacy

- **Roadmap:** ../ROADMAP.md · **Phase:** 2 · **Repo:** nesolva/docs · **Base branch:** release/public-docs-p2 (PR targets it, not main) · **Branch:** docs/ayuda
- **Depends on:** T-01 merged · **Model:** sonnet

## Goal
Anyone landing on docs.nesolva.com can see where to go. If something fails, they know whom to ask: their
organization's administrator or Nesolva. They can see what changed recently and understand which data is collected
and why, in plain language.

## Scope
- Files/areas: `es/index.mdx`, `es/ayuda/*.mdx`, five stubs from T-01.
- Out of scope: `docs.json` and the product pages.

## Contract
- Roadmap Decisions and Contracts.
- `es/index`:
  - cards to Ailita (for members and for administrators), Alfred, and Ayuda;
  - a "¿Qué necesitás?" block with direct links to "No puedo ingresar", "Perdí mi teléfono" and "Invitar a mi
    equipo".
- `ayuda/soporte`: who helps with what.
  - Your organization's administrator: access, roles, a lost second factor, a disabled account, invitations.
  - Nesolva (`support+docs@nesolva.com`): enabling products, integrations, the organization suspended, errors that
    persist.
  - What to include in a support request: product, approximate time, the exact message, and what you were doing.
    Never your password or codes.
  - If the status page URL is in the roadmap's Open questions as resolved, link it. If not, leave it out.
- `ayuda/preguntas-frecuentes`: 8–12 questions that link to the guides. For example: Can I delete my account? Why
  does it ask for a code on a new device? What is a passkey? Do I need an authenticator app?
- `ayuda/novedades`: Mintlify `<Update>` format, with one first entry, "Nuevo sitio de ayuda" (2026-11).
- `ayuda/datos-y-privacidad`:
  - which data is collected, at a policy level: account data (name, email, phone if you add it), the organization
    and roles;
  - security activity (sign-ins, devices, sessions, IP address, browser), so you and your administrator can review
    access and protect the account;
  - invoices and documents that Alfred processes for the organization;
  - where to see them in **Mi cuenta** (Actividad, Dispositivos, Sesiones, Aplicaciones), how to remove an app's
    access, and how to request deletion (through the administrator);
  - a link to https://nesolva.com/privacy as the legal source.

  Don't describe how this data is used to detect anything. This page waits for the legal review in the roadmap's
  Open questions: if it is still open, open the PR as a draft and say so under Result.
