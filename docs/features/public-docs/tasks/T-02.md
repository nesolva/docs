# T-02: Ailita, getting started and your account (end users)

- **Roadmap:** ../ROADMAP.md · **Phase:** 2 · **Repo:** nesolva/docs · **Base branch:** release/public-docs-p2 (PR targets it, not main) · **Branch:** docs/ailita-usuarios
- **Depends on:** T-01 merged · **Model:** sonnet

## Goal
A member of an organization understands what Ailita ID is and can create their account, sign in with whichever
method their organization uses, and manage their data, security, applications, devices and sessions without asking
anyone.

## Scope
- Files/areas:
  - `es/ailita/introduccion.mdx`, `es/ailita/crear-cuenta.mdx`, `es/ailita/iniciar-sesion.mdx`;
  - `es/ailita/cuenta/*.mdx`. These five are stubs that T-01 created.
- Source: SOURCE-FACTS.md, section A, except the messages table, which belongs to T-04.
- Out of scope: `docs.json`, the troubleshooting pages, recovery, and administration.

## Contract
- Roadmap Decisions and Contracts.
- Make it explicit that the available options depend on the organization: SSO, passkeys, email code, open sign-up,
  and a required second factor. Use `<Note>` for "if your organization…".
- Use `<Steps>` for flows. Use the UI labels in **bold**, exactly as SOURCE-FACTS.md has them.
- `iniciar-sesion`: cover every variant from section A, steps 1–11, including app consent and "Confirmá que sos vos".
- `cuenta/seguridad`: the authenticator app (with backup codes), passkeys and password. Recommend keeping the backup
  codes somewhere safe.
- `cuenta/actividad`: the event list, and what to do if you don't recognize an event.
- Say that account deletion is requested from the organization's administrator, and that there is no self-service
  option.
- Link to the troubleshooting and recovery pages (T-04) by their T-01 paths.
