Short answers to the most common questions. Each one takes you to the full guide.

## Can I delete my account?

Not from **My account**: there's no option to delete it yourself. Only an administrator of your organisation can delete a user. Ask your administrator. To find out what happens to your data, see [Data and privacy](/en/ayuda/datos-y-privacidad).

## Why does it ask me for a code when I sign in from a new device?

The first time you sign in from a device, we email you a code on the **Confirm it's you** screen. Enter it to continue. If it doesn't reach you, check your spam folder or use **Resend code**. More details in [Codes and second factor](/en/ailita/problemas/codigos-y-segundo-factor).

## What is a passkey?

A passkey lets you sign in with your device's unlock (your face, your finger or your PIN) instead of typing a password. You can create one when Ailita offers it or from **Security**, with **Add a passkey**. Your organisation can turn passkeys off. See [Security](/en/ailita/cuenta/seguridad).

## Do I need an authenticator app?

It depends on your organisation. If it requires a second factor, Ailita asks you to set it up the first time you sign in, with an app such as Google Authenticator, 1Password or Authy. Administrators always need a second factor. See [Security](/en/ailita/cuenta/seguridad).

## What do I do if I forgot my password?

On the sign-in screen, select **Forgot your password?** and follow the steps to change it. See [Forgot your password](/en/ailita/recuperar/contrasena).

## What do I do if I lost my phone?

If you don't have your authenticator app or your backup codes, select **Lost your phone and backup codes?** and send a request to your organisation. An administrator confirms it's you and sends you a link to set up a new second factor. See [I lost my second factor](/en/ailita/recuperar/segundo-factor).

## How do I sign out on other devices?

In **My account**, open **Sessions** and choose **End all other sessions**. You can also forget a device from **Devices**. See [Devices and sessions](/en/ailita/cuenta/dispositivos-y-sesiones).

## How do I remove an application's access to my account?

In **My account**, open **Applications**, choose **Review access** and then **Remove access**. The next time you use that application, you have to authorise it again. See [Connected apps](/en/ailita/cuenta/aplicaciones).

## How do I change my email?

In **My account**, open **Personal details** and use **Change email**. We send a code to the new address: enter it to confirm. See [Personal details](/en/ailita/cuenta/datos-personales).

## Why can't I create an account?

You can only create an account with an invitation link, or if your organisation allows open sign-up. If you see "This organisation only accepts invited users.", ask your administrator for an invitation. See [Create your account](/en/ailita/crear-cuenta) and [Account and invitations](/en/ailita/problemas/cuenta-e-invitaciones).

## Why does Alfred say it isn't enabled yet?

Your organisation doesn't have Alfred enabled yet. Ask Nesolva to enable it: write to us at [support+docs@nesolva.com](mailto:support+docs@nesolva.com). See [Sign in to Alfred](/en/alfred/ingresar).

## Why can't I claim a task in Alfred?

To claim tasks you need the **Operator** role. Ask your administrator to assign it to you. See [Review a task](/en/alfred/revisar-tarea).
