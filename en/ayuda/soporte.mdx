Depending on what's happening, either your organisation's administrator or Nesolva can help you. Your administrator solves most access issues.

## Talk to your organisation's administrator

Your administrator can help you with:

- Your **access**: which products you can use and how you sign in.
- Your **roles** and permissions.
- A **lost second factor**: if you don't have your phone or your backup codes, ask for help from the two-step verification screen. See [I lost my second factor](/en/ailita/recuperar/segundo-factor).
- A **disabled account**: if you see "This account is disabled. Talk to your administrator.", only your administrator can reactivate it.
- **Invitations**: to join the organisation, or to get a new one if yours is no longer valid.

If you don't know who manages your organisation, ask whoever invited you.

## Write to Nesolva

Write to us at [support+docs@nesolva.com](mailto:support+docs@nesolva.com) if you need help with:

- **Enabling products** in your organisation, for example Alfred or purchase orders.
- **Integrations** with other systems.
- A **suspended organisation**.
- An **error that keeps happening** even though you tried what the [troubleshooting](/en/ailita/problemas/iniciar-sesion) guide says.

## What to include in your request

A clear request gets solved faster. Include:

1. **The product**: Ailita ID, Ailita Backoffice or Alfred.
2. **The approximate time** it happened.
3. **The exact message** you saw on screen.
4. **What you were doing** when it appeared.

<Warning>
  Never include your password or codes: not the ones from your email, not the ones from your authenticator app, and not your backup codes.
</Warning>
