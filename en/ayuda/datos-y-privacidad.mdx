This page explains, in plain words, what data we keep when you use Ailita and Alfred, what we use it for and where you can see it. The legal source is the [privacy policy](https://nesolva.com/privacy).

## What data we keep

### Your account data

- Your first and last name, and your email.
- Your phone number, if you add it. It's optional.
- The organisation you belong to and your roles in it.

We use it to identify you, show you your organisation and give you the permissions that apply to you.

### Security activity

- Your sign-ins and sign-outs.
- Your devices and open sessions.
- The IP address and browser of each sign-in.

We keep it so that you and your organisation's administrator can review who accesses the account and protect it.

### Alfred invoices and documents

Alfred processes the invoices and documents your organisation receives or that you upload. We use them to extract their data, make it available to your team and keep the history of every change.

## Where to see it

In **My account** you can review your data and your activity:

| Section | What you see |
|---|---|
| **Personal details** | Your first name, last name, phone and email. You can edit them. |
| **Activity** | Your recent events: sign-ins, password changes, new devices and more. |
| **Devices** | The devices you signed in from. You can forget the ones you don't use. |
| **Sessions** | Your open sessions. You can end one or all the others. |
| **Applications** | The connected applications and the permissions you gave them. |

If you see something you don't recognise in **Activity**, change your password, end your other sessions and tell your administrator.

## Remove an application's access

The first time an application asks you for data, you see what it will be able to use and decide whether to allow it. To remove its access later:

1. In **My account**, open **Applications**.
2. Choose **Review access** on the application.
3. Choose **Remove access**.

The next time, you have to authorise the application again. See [Connected apps](/en/ailita/cuenta/aplicaciones).

## Ask for your data to be deleted

Today there's no option to delete your account from **My account**. Ask your administrator to delete your user. For other questions about your data, write to us at [support+docs@nesolva.com](mailto:support+docs@nesolva.com) or see the [privacy policy](https://nesolva.com/privacy).
