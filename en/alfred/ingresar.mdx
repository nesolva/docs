Alfred has no username or password of its own: you sign in with your Ailita account. Whoever manages your organisation manages access in Ailita.

## Before you start

- You need an Ailita account in your organisation. If you don't have one yet, see [Create your account](/en/ailita/crear-cuenta).
- Whoever manages your organisation must have assigned you the **Operator** role. Without that role you can sign in, but you can't claim tasks.
- Your organisation must have Alfred enabled. See [What is Alfred](/en/alfred/introduccion).

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Alfred">
    On the **Sign in to Alfred** screen you'll see the **Sign in with Ailita** button.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go in with Ailita">
    Select **Sign in with Ailita**. Alfred takes you to the Ailita sign-in screen.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in as usual">
    Complete the sign-in with the method your organisation uses. If you need help with this step, see [Sign in](/en/ailita/iniciar-sesion).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Return to Alfred">
    When you finish, you return to Alfred and see the **Processing** dashboard.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Your account and how to sign out

From the user menu you can:

- Open **Ailita account** to view and change your account details.
- Select **Sign out** to leave Alfred.

## If you can't sign in

See the sign-in messages in [Alfred troubleshooting](/en/alfred/problemas). For example, if you see that your organisation doesn't have Alfred enabled yet, ask Nesolva to enable it. If the problem is with your Ailita account, see [Sign-in problems](/en/ailita/problemas/iniciar-sesion).
