Alfred extracts, validates and routes the invoices your team receives. It reads the PDFs that arrive in your organisation's billing mailbox, flags what needs a person's review and keeps a history of every change.

If your organisation has it enabled, you can also build the file for each [purchase order](/en/alfred/ordenes-de-compra).

## What your organisation needs to get started

Before you sign in, check these three points:

1. **Alfred is enabled for your organisation.** If it isn't yet, ask Nesolva to enable it.
2. **Whoever manages your organisation assigned you the Operator role.** With that role you can process documents, download the documents' PDFs and files, and read invoices and download their PDFs.
3. **The billing mailbox is connected.** Whoever manages your organisation connects it from Ailita Backoffice. See how in [Billing mailbox](/en/ailita/admin/casilla-de-facturacion).

## How you sign in

Alfred uses your Ailita account: it has no username or password of its own. Whoever manages your organisation manages access in Ailita. See [Sign in to Alfred](/en/alfred/ingresar).
