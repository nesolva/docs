Every invoice Alfred receives creates a **task**. The dashboard shows you all the tasks and the status each one is in.

## What the dashboard shows

When you sign in to Alfred, under **Processing**, you see the **Recent tasks** list. Each row has:

- the task,
- the status,
- the document,
- when it was received.

To narrow the list, use the filters for **status**, **Desde** (from) and **Hasta** (to).

To open a task, select it. You'll see **Task #** followed by the task's number.

## What each status means

| Status | What it means | What you do |
|---|---|---|
| **PENDING** | Alfred received the invoice and hasn't started processing it yet. | Nothing. Wait. |
| **IN_PROGRESS** | Alfred is reading and validating the invoice. | Nothing. Check again in a while. |
| **MANUAL_REVIEW** | Alfred needs a person to review the data. | Claim the task, review the fields and finalise it. See [Review a task](/en/alfred/revisar-tarea). |
| **FINALIZED** | A person reviewed and closed the task. | Nothing, unless you find a mistake: you can claim it again and correct it. |
| **FAILED** | Alfred couldn't process the invoice. | Upload the invoice again from [Upload an invoice](/en/alfred/subir-factura). If it fails again, [ask for help](/en/ayuda/soporte). |

<Note>
  Alfred's English screens show each status as a code, exactly as listed in the table above, instead of a name.
</Note>

## Which tasks are yours

Tasks in **MANUAL_REVIEW** are the ones that need your work. To claim one, you need the **Operator** role. If you don't have it, ask whoever manages your organisation to assign it.

If someone else already claimed the task, Alfred tells you. See the messages in [Troubleshooting](/en/alfred/problemas).
