In **Invoices** you find the invoices Alfred has already processed and see the details of each one.

## Before you start

The **Operator** role includes reading invoices and downloading their PDFs. If you don't see the section or can't download the PDF, ask whoever manages your organisation to review your role.

## Search for an invoice

You can search by:

- supplier,
- RUT or tax ID,
- purchase order,
- country,
- invoice date,
- total amount,
- category.

Some searches have conditions:

- **Total amount:** also choose a currency. If you don't, Alfred shows **Choose a currency to filter by amount.**
- **Date:** if you search by date alone, pick a range of one month at most or add another filter. If you don't, Alfred shows **Pick a range of one month at most, or add a … filter.**

## Choose the view

You can see the results in two ways:

- **Cards**: one card per invoice.
- **Table**: one row per invoice.

## See an invoice's details

Select an invoice to open its details. There you see:

- the supplier and the buyer;
- the invoice's line items;
- the addenda;
- the extracted fields, each with its confidence. If the confidence is low, look at the field closely.

If the invoice is still in review, **Open review task** appears. Select it to go to the task. See [Review a task](/en/alfred/revisar-tarea).

From there you can also download the invoice's PDF.

## If something doesn't work

If the PDF doesn't open, see **Failed to load PDF** in [Troubleshooting](/en/alfred/problemas).
