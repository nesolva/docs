Find the message you see on screen. Each entry says what happened, what to do and where to go if it keeps happening.

<Note>
  If the problem is a code that doesn't arrive or doesn't work, see [Codes and second factor](/en/ailita/problemas/codigos-y-segundo-factor). If it's an invitation or a change to your details, see [Account and invitations](/en/ailita/problemas/cuenta-e-invitaciones).
</Note>

## Email or password

### Wrong email or password.

**Why:** the email or the password doesn't match an account.

**What to do:** check that the email is typed correctly and try again. If you don't remember the password, [recover it with a code by email](/en/ailita/recuperar/contrasena).

**Still stuck:** [ask for help](/en/ayuda/soporte).

### Too many attempts. Wait a few minutes.

**Why:** you made several attempts in a row.

**What to do:** wait a few minutes and try again. If you don't remember the password, [recover it](/en/ailita/recuperar/contrasena) instead of trying again.

**Still stuck:** [ask for help](/en/ayuda/soporte).

## Your sign-in was left halfway

### Your sign-in expired. Start again.

**Why:** you left the sign-in screen open too long before finishing.

**What to do:** go back to the app you wanted to sign in to, or open Ailita again, and start over.

**Still stuck:** [ask for help](/en/ayuda/soporte).

### Your sign-in changed in another tab. Start again.

**Why:** you started signing in in more than one tab or window and one overwrote the other.

**What to do:** close the other sign-in tabs and start again in a single one.

**Still stuck:** [ask for help](/en/ayuda/soporte).

### Request rejected. Reload the page.

**Why:** we couldn't validate the request, for example because the page was open for a long time.

**What to do:** reload the page and try again.

**Still stuck:** [ask for help](/en/ayuda/soporte).

### We couldn't connect. Check your connection.

**Why:** the page lost its connection to Ailita.

**What to do:** check your internet connection, reload the page and try again.

**Still stuck:** [ask for help](/en/ayuda/soporte).

### Something went wrong. Please try again.

**Why:** there was an error that isn't any of the other messages.

**What to do:** try again. If it appears again, wait a while and retry.

**Still stuck:** [ask for help](/en/ayuda/soporte) and tell us what you were doing.

## Account or organisation

### This account is disabled. Talk to your administrator.

**Why:** an administrator of your organisation disabled your account.

**What to do:** talk to your administrator. When they reactivate your account, you'll be able to sign in as usual.

**Still stuck:** if your administrator already reactivated it and you still see the message, [ask for help](/en/ayuda/soporte).

### Your organisation is suspended.

**Why:** the organisation you belong to is suspended and nobody can sign in in the meantime.

**What to do:** talk to your administrator.

**Still stuck:** [ask for help](/en/ayuda/soporte).

### We blocked this sign-in for your security. Try from a device you usually use.

**Why:** it's a precaution to protect your account.

**What to do:** try again from a device you usually use.

**Still stuck:** talk to your administrator. After you sign in, you can review your recent activity in **My account** → **Activity**.

## Signing in with a passkey

### We couldn't verify the passkey.

**Why:** the check with your passkey didn't complete.

**What to do:** try again with **Sign in with a passkey**, or sign in another way, for example with your password.

**Still stuck:** [ask for help](/en/ayuda/soporte).

### The request expired. Try again.

**Why:** the prompt for your passkey took too long to complete.

**What to do:** try again and answer your device's prompt right away (face, finger or PIN).

**Still stuck:** sign in another way. If you can't, [ask for help](/en/ayuda/soporte).

### Your organisation doesn't allow passkeys.

**Why:** your organisation turned passkeys off.

**What to do:** sign in another way, for example with your password or an email code.

**Still stuck:** talk to your administrator.

## Signing in with Google or Microsoft

### You cancelled the sign-in with your provider.

**Why:** you closed or cancelled the Google or Microsoft screen before finishing.

**What to do:** try again and complete the sign-in with your provider.

**Still stuck:** [ask for help](/en/ayuda/soporte).

### The sign-in with your provider expired. Try again.

**Why:** signing in with Google or Microsoft took too long.

**What to do:** start again and complete it without delay.

**Still stuck:** [ask for help](/en/ayuda/soporte).

### We couldn't complete the sign-in with your provider.

**Why:** there was an error signing in with Google or Microsoft.

**What to do:** try again.

**Still stuck:** [ask for help](/en/ayuda/soporte).

### Your provider account doesn't have access to this organisation.

**Why:** the Google or Microsoft account you signed in with wasn't invited to this organisation.

**What to do:** ask your administrator for an invitation.

**Still stuck:** [ask for help](/en/ayuda/soporte).

### Your email isn't allowed for this organisation.

**Why:** the email you used isn't one of those allowed for this organisation.

**What to do:** sign in with your work email.

**Still stuck:** talk to your administrator.

### Your Microsoft account belongs to another organisation.

**Why:** you signed in with a Microsoft account that isn't your work account.

**What to do:** sign in with your work Microsoft account. If you have several accounts open in the browser, choose the right one when Microsoft asks.

**Still stuck:** talk to your administrator.

### The app asked you to sign in again, but your provider didn't ask for your password again. Try again.

**Why:** the app asked you to sign in again, but Google or Microsoft carried on with your previous session.

**What to do:** try again from the app.

**Still stuck:** [ask for help](/en/ayuda/soporte).

## We can't continue

If an app sends you to sign in and something fails, you see the **We can't continue** screen with one of these messages. All three are solved the same way: go back to the app and try again.

### The sign-in link is incomplete or was modified. Go back to the app and try again.

**Why:** the sign-in link you opened is incomplete or was modified.

**What to do:** go back to the app and start the sign-in from there. Don't use a link copied or saved from an earlier time.

**Still stuck:** contact the app's support.

### This app isn't registered with Ailita.

**Why:** the app that sent you to sign in isn't registered with Ailita.

**What to do:** go back to the app and try again.

**Still stuck:** contact the app's support.

### This app isn't allowed to receive your sign-in at that address.

**Why:** the app can't receive your sign-in at that address.

**What to do:** go back to the app and try again.

**Still stuck:** contact the app's support.

## Your session ends

If Ailita or an app asks you to sign in again when you don't expect it, there are a few possible reasons:

- Your organisation may have chosen a maximum session length or an idle timeout. When it's reached, you have to sign in again.
- Someone ended your sessions, for example after a password change or a security change on your account.
- You signed out of the app or of **My account**. In that case you see **You signed out** and the option **Sign in again**.

**What to do:** select **Sign in again** or open the app again and sign in. If it happens often, ask your administrator which session rules your organisation uses.

If you didn't change anything and weren't expecting this sign-out, go to **My account** → **Activity**. If you see something you don't recognise, change your password, end your other sessions and tell your administrator.
