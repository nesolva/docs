Find the message you see when you create your account, accept an invitation or change your details.

## Create an account and invitations

### This organisation only accepts invited users.

**Why:** in this organisation you can only create an account with an invitation.

**What to do:** ask your administrator for an invitation and use the link you receive by email.

**Still stuck:** [ask for help](/en/ayuda/soporte).

### This invitation is no longer valid.

**Why:** the invitation was already used, was cancelled or expired. Invitations expire after a few days.

**What to do:** ask your administrator for a new invitation. If you already created your account with that invitation, sign in with your email and password.

**Still stuck:** [ask for help](/en/ayuda/soporte).

### Use the email the invitation was sent to.

**Why:** you tried to create the account with a different email from the one that received the invitation.

**What to do:** use the email the invitation was sent to. When you open the link, the email already appears fixed on **Create your account**.

**Still stuck:** ask your administrator to invite the email you want to use.

### Confirm your email first. We sent you a new link.

**Why:** you haven't confirmed your email yet.

**What to do:** open the new link we sent you. When you see "Email confirmed. You can sign in now.", sign in with your email and password. If you can't find it, check your spam folder.

**Still stuck:** [ask for help](/en/ayuda/soporte).

## Password

### The password doesn't meet the requirements.

**Why:** the password is too weak or too short.

**What to do:** choose a longer phrase. A long phrase beats odd symbols.

**Still stuck:** [ask for help](/en/ayuda/soporte).

### Passwords don't match

**Why:** the two password fields are different.

**What to do:** type the same password in **New password** and in **Repeat the password**.

**Still stuck:** [ask for help](/en/ayuda/soporte).

### Your current password is wrong.

**Why:** when changing your password in **My account**, the current password you typed doesn't match.

**What to do:** check what you typed. If you don't remember your current password, [recover it with a code by email](/en/ailita/recuperar/contrasena).

**Still stuck:** [ask for help](/en/ayuda/soporte).

## Change your email

### That email is already used in your organisation.

**Why:** another account in your organisation already uses that email.

**What to do:** use another email in **New email**.

**Still stuck:** talk to your administrator.

### The email-change code doesn't arrive

When you select **Change email**, we send a code to the new email, not the old one. Check your spam folder and confirm the address is typed correctly. If it still doesn't arrive, [ask for help](/en/ayuda/soporte).

## Confirm it's you

### Confirm it's you to continue.

**Why:** before a sensitive change, Ailita asks you to confirm your identity.

**What to do:**

1. On **Confirm it's you**, choose how to confirm: with a code from your authenticator app, with a code by email or with **Confirm with a passkey**.
2. You then return to the same page. Repeat the change.

If you don't have a second factor, Ailita asks you to sign in again. After you sign in, repeat the change.

**Still stuck:** if the code doesn't work, see [Codes and second factor](/en/ailita/problemas/codigos-y-segundo-factor). Otherwise, [ask for help](/en/ayuda/soporte).
