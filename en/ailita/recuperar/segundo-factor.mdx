If you lost your phone and your backup codes too, you can't complete **Two-step verification**. In that case, an administrator of your organisation confirms it's you and sends you a link to start over.

<Tip>
  Before asking for help, check whether you still have a backup code. Each 8-character code works once. You can type it on **Two-step verification** instead of the app's code.
</Tip>

## Have at hand

- Your password. If you don't remember it, [recover it](/en/ailita/recuperar/contrasena) first.
- Access to your email, because the link arrives there.
- A way for your administrator to reach you, for example a call or a video call. They confirm your identity through another channel before approving the request.
- A phone with an authenticator app, to set up the new second factor at the end.

## Ask for recovery

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start the request">
    On the **Two-step verification** screen, select **Lost your phone and backup codes?**. **Ask your organisation for help** opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write a message (optional)">
    In **Message for your administrator** you can add details that help them recognise you, or a way to contact you. Select **Send request**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait to be contacted">
    You see **Request sent**. An administrator of your organisation contacts you and confirms your identity through another channel, for example a call or a video call.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the link">
    When the administrator approves the request, you get an email with a link. The link works once and is valid for a limited time. When you open it, you see **Recover access**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Remove the old second factor">
    Type your password and select **Remove the old second factor**. We end all your sessions.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set up a new one">
    Sign in again. On **Protect your account**, scan the QR code with an authenticator app such as Google Authenticator, 1Password or Authy. If you can't scan, use the **Can't scan? Enter this key:** option and type the code the app shows.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the new backup codes">
    On **Save your backup codes**, select **Copy** and keep them somewhere safe. They're shown only once. Then select **I saved them**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## If your administrator can't approve the request

- **They rejected the request.** The administrator can leave a reason, and you see it on the screen. Talk to that person.
- **The request expired.** Ask for recovery again from **Lost your phone and backup codes?**.
- **The link no longer works.** Ask for recovery again.

Your administrator can also reset your second factor from their console. In that case you set up a new one the next time you sign in.

<Note>
  If you can't sign in for another reason, for example a forgotten password, recovering the second factor doesn't solve it. See [I can't sign in](/en/ailita/problemas/iniciar-sesion).
</Note>
