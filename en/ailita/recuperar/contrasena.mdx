If you don't remember your password, you can choose a new one with a code sent to your email. You don't need to ask anyone for help.

<Note>
  This page is about your password. If the problem is your phone or your backup codes, see [I lost my second factor](/en/ailita/recuperar/segundo-factor).
</Note>

## Before you start

- Have at hand the email you use to sign in to Ailita, and access to that inbox.
- If you sign in with Google or Microsoft, your organisation uses corporate sign-in and you don't have a Nesolva password to recover. Sign in with **Continuar con Google** or **Continuar con Microsoft** (Continue with Google, Continue with Microsoft).
- Your administrator can turn off password recovery by email. If you can't use this route, ask your administrator for help.

## Change your password

<Steps>
  <Step title="Ask for the code">
    On the **Good to see you.** screen, type your email and select **Continue**. On **Your password**, select **Forgot your password?**.

    **Reset your password** opens. Confirm your email and select **Send code**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check your email">
    If there's an account with that email, we send you a 6-digit code. The email also has a link that opens the same form.

    If the code doesn't arrive, see [Codes and second factor](/en/ailita/problemas/codigos-y-segundo-factor).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the new password">
    Fill in the fields:

    - **Código** (code): the code we sent you.
    - **New password**: a long phrase beats odd symbols.
    - **Repeat the password**: the same password again.

    Select **Change password**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in with the new password">
    You see **Password updated**. For your security, we sign you out on all your other devices. Sign in again with the new password.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## If something goes wrong

| You see | What to do |
|---|---|
| That code is wrong or has expired. | Ask for a new code from **Reset your password**. |
| The password doesn't meet the requirements. | Choose a longer phrase. |
| Passwords don't match | Type the same password in both fields. |

To see these messages in full and all the others, see [I can't sign in](/en/ailita/problemas/iniciar-sesion), [Codes and second factor](/en/ailita/problemas/codigos-y-segundo-factor) and [Account and invitations](/en/ailita/problemas/cuenta-e-invitaciones).
