In **My account**, the **Activity** section shows the recent events on your account. Check it from time to time to confirm you recognise every event.

## Which events appear

- **Signed in**
- **Failed sign-in attempt**
- **Sign-in blocked**
- **Signed out**
- **New device**
- **Device forgotten**
- **Authenticator app turned on** and **Authenticator app turned off**
- **Passkey added** and **Passkey removed**
- **Password changed**
- **Password reset**
- **Email changed**
- **App connected** and **App disconnected**
- **App session revoked for security**
- **Security event**

## If you don't recognise an event

<Steps>
  <Step title="Change your password">
    See [Security](/en/ailita/cuenta/seguridad). If you don't remember your password, see [Forgot your password](/en/ailita/recuperar/contrasena).
  </Step>
  <Step title="End your other sessions">
    In [Devices and sessions](/en/ailita/cuenta/dispositivos-y-sesiones), select **End all other sessions**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tell your administrator">
    Tell them which event you saw and when.
  </Step>
</Steps>
