In **My account**, the **Applications** section lists the applications that use your Ailita account. For each one you see the permissions you gave it, when it was connected and when you last used it.

The first time an application asks for your information, you decide what to share. See [Sign in](/en/ailita/iniciar-sesion).

## Review or remove an application's access

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Applications">
    Go to **My account** and choose **Applications**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the access">
    Select **Review access** on the application you want to look at. Its permissions are shown.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Remove the access">
    If you no longer want the application to use your account, select **Remove access**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

After you remove access, you authorise the application again the next time you use it.

## Which permissions an application can ask for

- Recognise you when you come back.
- Your first and last name.
- Your email address.
- The organisation you belong to.
- Your roles in the organisation.
- Keep you signed in while you use it.

<Note>
If you see an application you don't recognise, remove its access and tell your administrator.
</Note>
