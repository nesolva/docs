In **My account** there are two sections for reviewing where you signed in: **Devices** and **Sessions**.

## Devices

**Devices** lists the devices you've signed in from. Each one can have these tags:

- **This device**: the one you're using now.
- **Trusted**: the device is listed as trusted.

Select **View details** to see when you first signed in, your last activity and the IP address.

To remove a device from the list, select **Forget**. You can do this with any device except the current one.

## Sessions

**Sessions** lists the open sessions on your account. The one you're using has the **Current session** tag.

Select **Review session** to see when it started, its last activity, the browser and the IP address.

- **End** ends that session.
- **End all other sessions** ends all of them except the current one.

## If you don't recognise a device or a session

<Steps>
  <Step title="End the sessions">
    Select **End all other sessions**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Change your password">
    See [Security](/en/ailita/cuenta/seguridad).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tell your administrator">
    Tell them what you saw so they can review your account.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To see the history of sign-ins and changes, see [Activity](/en/ailita/cuenta/actividad).
