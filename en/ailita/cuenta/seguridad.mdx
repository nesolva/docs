In **My account**, the **Security** section lets you look after how you sign in. At the top you see the status of your account: either two-step verification is enabled, or an authenticator app is not set up yet.

<Note>
What you see here depends on your organisation. It can turn passkeys off, and the password row only appears on accounts that have a password.
</Note>

## Authenticator app

An authenticator app generates a code each time you sign in. It works as a second factor.

### Set it up

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select Set up">
    Under **Authenticator app**, select **Set up**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Scan the QR code">
    Use Google Authenticator, 1Password, Authy or a similar app. If you can't scan, use the key shown on the screen.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Type the code from the app">
    Enter the code the app shows to confirm.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save your backup codes">
    Copy the codes and keep them somewhere safe, for example in your password manager. Each code works once, and you only see them this time.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Backup codes

Backup codes let you sign in if you don't have the app at hand. While the app is on, you see how many codes you have left.

- **Generate new codes** creates a new set.
- Keep the new codes somewhere safe. If you lose your phone and your codes, see [I lost my second factor](/en/ailita/recuperar/segundo-factor).

### Turn it off

Select **Turn off** under **Authenticator app**. If your organisation requires a second factor, you see the message **Your organisation requires a second factor.** and you have to set one up.

## Passkeys

A passkey lets you sign in with your device's face, finger or PIN, without typing your password.

- To add one, select **Add a passkey** and type a name that helps you recognise it.
- The list shows when you created each passkey and when you last used it.
- To remove one, select **Remove**.

If your organisation doesn't allow passkeys, you see the message **Your organisation doesn't allow passkeys.** Use another way to sign in.

## Password

Under **Password**, type your current password and the new one. When you change it, you're signed out on your other devices.

A long phrase beats odd symbols.

If you see **Your current password is wrong.**, check it or recover it with [Forgot your password](/en/ailita/recuperar/contrasena).

## Active sessions

The link to active sessions takes you to [Devices and sessions](/en/ailita/cuenta/dispositivos-y-sesiones).

## Confirm before a change

Before a sensitive change, Ailita may show you **Confirm it's you**. Confirm and repeat the change. See [Sign in](/en/ailita/iniciar-sesion).
