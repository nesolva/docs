In **My account**, the **Personal details** section shows your account details and lets you change them.

## Change your name or your phone

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Personal details">
    Go to **My account** and choose **Personal details**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Edit my details">
    Change your first name, your last name or your phone number.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save your changes">
    Confirm and check that the new details appear on the screen.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
Your phone number is only a detail on your account. Ailita doesn't verify it or use it to sign you in.
</Note>

## Change your email

<Steps>
  <Step title="Type your new email">
    In **New email**, type the new address and select **Change email**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the code">
    We send a code to the new address. Type it and select **Continue**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the mark">
    A confirmed address is marked **verified**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If you're asked **Confirm it's you to continue.**, confirm and repeat the change. See [Sign in](/en/ailita/iniciar-sesion).

If you see **That email is already used in your organisation.**, use another address.

## Delete your account

You can't delete your account from Ailita. To delete it, ask an administrator of your organisation.
