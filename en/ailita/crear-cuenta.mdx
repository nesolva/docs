You can create your account in two ways: with an invitation link from your organisation, or from the sign-in screen when your organisation allows sign-up.

<Note>
If your organisation only accepts invited users, you need the invitation to create your account. Without one, the screen tells you that to create an account you need a link from your organisation or an invitation. Ask an administrator for it.
</Note>

## Create your account from an invitation

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the invitation link">
    The **Create your account** screen appears, with a note that your organisation invited you to join.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in your details">
    Fill in **Nombre** (first name), **Apellido** (last name) and **Contraseña** (password). **Email** is already filled in and you can't change it.

    For the password, a long phrase beats odd symbols.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Create account">
    You'll see one of two screens, depending on your organisation:

    - **Check your email**: confirm your email with the link we sent you.
    - **Your account is ready**: you can sign in now.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm your email (if asked)">
    Open the link in the email. **Confirming your email…** appears, followed by **Email confirmed. You can sign in now.** From there, continue with [Sign in](/en/ailita/iniciar-sesion).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Create your account without an invitation

If your organisation allows sign-up, the sign-in screen shows Don't have an account? with the **Create account** link. Select **Create account** and follow the same steps as above. In this case you type your own email.

## If something doesn't work

- **This invitation is no longer valid.**: ask your administrator for a new one.
- **Use the email the invitation was sent to.**: type the email the invitation was sent to.
- **This organisation only accepts invited users.**: ask for an invitation.

To see the messages and what to do about them, see [Account and invitations](/en/ailita/problemas/cuenta-e-invitaciones).
