The billing mailbox is the email inbox where your organisation's invoices arrive. [Alfred](/en/alfred/introduccion) reads that mailbox to capture the invoices. To connect it, your organisation needs a [verified domain](/en/ailita/admin/dominios-y-sso) and a Microsoft mailbox.

## Connect the mailbox

<Steps>
  <Step title="Save the address">
    In **Billing mailbox**, type the mailbox address and save.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect it with Microsoft">
    Select **Connect with Microsoft**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in with the mailbox's account">
    Sign in with the **mailbox's** account, not your own. If you sign in with another account, the Backoffice responds "You signed in with another account. You must sign in with the mailbox's account."
  </Step>
  <Step title="Accept all the permissions">
    Microsoft shows you a list of permissions. Accept them all. If you don't, the Backoffice responds "Microsoft didn't grant permanent access. Accept every permission."
  </Step>
</Steps>

When it finishes, the Backoffice tells you "Mailbox connected. Alfred starts reading new invoices."

## Mailbox statuses

| Status | What to do |
|---|---|
| **Connected** | Nothing. Alfred is reading the mailbox. |
| **Not connected yet** | Follow the steps above. |
| **Disconnected** | Reconnect the mailbox. |

## Reconnect the mailbox

Sometimes Microsoft stops accepting the access, and the mailbox changes to **Disconnected**. Alfred doesn't read new invoices until you reconnect it.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Reconnect">
    In **Billing mailbox**, select **Reconnect with Microsoft**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in again">
    Sign in with the mailbox's account and accept all the permissions, as when you connected it.
  </Step>
</Steps>
