When someone loses their phone and their backup codes, they can't complete two-step verification. That person asks you for help and you decide whether to give it. The requests appear in **Access recovery**.

## How a request arrives

The person selects **Lost your phone and backup codes?** on the verification screen and sends a request, with an optional message for you. You can see what they see in [I lost my second factor](/en/ailita/recuperar/segundo-factor).

## Before approving: confirm their identity

Before approving, confirm that the person is who they say they are, through a channel other than the one they used to ask for help: a call, a video call or in person. It's good practice for any organisation. The Backoffice also asks you to: without that confirmation it responds "Confirm the person's identity through another channel before approving."

## Approve a request

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the request">
    In **Access recovery**, choose the request and select **Approve…**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the identity">
    Tick that you confirmed the identity through another channel (call, video call or in person). If you want, leave an internal note.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send the link">
    Select **Approve and send link**. The person receives a one-time link by email, valid for a limited time.
  </Step>
</Steps>

With that link, the person confirms their password, removes their old second factor and sets up a new one when they sign in again. All their sessions are closed.

## Reject a request

Select **Reject…**. You can write a reason, and the person sees it. If you couldn't confirm their identity, reject the request and explain how to proceed.

## Request statuses

| Status | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Pending** | Waiting for your decision. |
| **Approved · waiting for the user** | You sent the link and the person hasn't used it yet. |
| **Rejected** | You rejected the request. |
| **Completed** | The person recovered access. |
| **Expired** | The request expired. If the Backoffice responds "The request expired: the person must ask again.", the person has to send another one. |

A request you've already decided can't be decided again: the Backoffice responds "That request was already decided."

## Another option: reset the second factor

You can also [reset a person's second factor](/en/ailita/admin/usuarios-e-invitaciones) from their page in **Users**. First, confirm their identity just as above.
