Find the message you see on screen. Each one explains why it appears and what to do. If the problem is with someone in your organisation who can't sign in, see [I can't sign in](/en/ailita/problemas/iniciar-sesion).

## Signing in to the Backoffice

### We couldn't sign you in

- **Symptom:** the **We couldn't sign you in** screen, with the option to retry.
- **Why:** signing in to the Backoffice failed.
- **What to do:** select **Retry** on that screen. If the message that comes with the screen is another one on this list, follow what it says there.
- **Still stuck:** [write to us](/en/ayuda/soporte).

### Your account is not an administrator of any organisation. Ask whoever manages yours for access.

- **Symptom:** when you enter, you see this message.
- **Why:** your account doesn't administer any organisation.
- **What to do:** ask an administrator of your organisation to give you access. See [Roles](/en/ailita/admin/roles).
- **Still stuck:** [write to us](/en/ayuda/soporte).

### The sign-in was cancelled or that account has no access.

- **Symptom:** when you sign in to the Backoffice, you see this message.
- **Why:** you cancelled the sign-in, or the account you signed in with has no access.
- **What to do:** try again and sign in with your administration account.
- **Still stuck:** [write to us](/en/ayuda/soporte).

### We couldn't complete the sign-in. Please try again.

- **Symptom:** the sign-in doesn't finish and you see this message.
- **Why:** the sign-in couldn't be completed.
- **What to do:** try again from the beginning.
- **Still stuck:** [write to us](/en/ayuda/soporte).

### Your account has no administration permissions.

- **Symptom:** you enter the Backoffice, but you don't see any section.
- **Why:** your account has no administration permissions.
- **What to do:** ask another administrator to review your roles. See [Roles](/en/ailita/admin/roles).
- **Still stuck:** [write to us](/en/ayuda/soporte).

### Your session ended. Please sign in again.

- **Symptom:** the Backoffice takes you out of the screen you were on and you see this message.
- **Why:** your session ended.
- **What to do:** sign in again and repeat what you were doing.
- **Still stuck:** [write to us](/en/ayuda/soporte).

## Permissions and requests

### You don't have permission to do this.

- **Symptom:** when you try a change, you see this message.
- **Why:** your account doesn't have the permission that action needs.
- **What to do:** ask another administrator to make the change or to assign you the right role. See [Roles](/en/ailita/admin/roles).
- **Still stuck:** [write to us](/en/ayuda/soporte).

### We couldn't validate the request. Reload the page and try again.

- **Symptom:** when you save or confirm, you see this message.
- **Why:** the Backoffice couldn't validate your request.
- **What to do:** reload the page and try again.
- **Still stuck:** [write to us](/en/ayuda/soporte).

### Too many attempts. Wait a few minutes.

- **Symptom:** you see this message after repeating an action several times.
- **Why:** you did the same action many times in a short while.
- **What to do:** wait a few minutes and try again.
- **Still stuck:** [write to us](/en/ayuda/soporte).

### Check the highlighted fields.

- **Symptom:** a form doesn't save and some fields are highlighted.
- **Why:** some details are missing or not valid.
- **What to do:** fix the highlighted fields and save again.
- **Still stuck:** [write to us](/en/ayuda/soporte).

### Something went wrong. Please try again.

- **Symptom:** you see this message and no more details.
- **Why:** something unexpected happened.
- **What to do:** try again. If you see the message again, note what you were doing and write to us.
- **Still stuck:** [write to us](/en/ayuda/soporte).

## Users and invitations

### That person already has an account.

- **Symptom:** when you invite someone, you see this message.
- **Why:** the email already has an account.
- **What to do:** you don't need to invite them. Find them in **Users** and review their status and roles. See [Users and invitations](/en/ailita/admin/usuarios-e-invitaciones).
- **Still stuck:** [write to us](/en/ayuda/soporte).

### You can't do that to your own account.

- **Symptom:** when you disable or delete your own account, you see this message.
- **Why:** you can't disable or delete yourself.
- **What to do:** ask another administrator to do it.
- **Still stuck:** [write to us](/en/ayuda/soporte).

## Roles

### A role with that name already exists.

- **Symptom:** when you create a role, you see this message.
- **Why:** your organisation already has a role with that name.
- **What to do:** choose another name.
- **Still stuck:** [write to us](/en/ayuda/soporte).

### You can only grant permissions you hold yourself…

- **Symptom:** when you create or change a role, you see this message.
- **Why:** the role includes a permission your account doesn't have. You can only grant permissions you hold.
- **What to do:** remove that permission from the role, or ask someone who holds it to make the change.
- **Still stuck:** [write to us](/en/ayuda/soporte).

### At least one active person must keep the permission to manage roles…

- **Symptom:** when you change a person's roles, you see this message.
- **Why:** the change would leave the organisation with no active person who can manage roles.
- **What to do:** assign that permission to another active person before you make the change. See [Roles](/en/ailita/admin/roles).
- **Still stuck:** [write to us](/en/ayuda/soporte).

## Domains and SSO

### Invalid domain.

- **Symptom:** when you add a domain, you see this message.
- **Why:** what you typed isn't a valid domain.
- **What to do:** check that you typed it correctly, with no typos.
- **Still stuck:** [write to us](/en/ayuda/soporte).

### Public mail domains… can't belong to an organisation.

- **Symptom:** when you add a domain, you see this message (public mail domains such as gmail.com or outlook.com).
- **Why:** public mail domains can't be linked to an organisation.
- **What to do:** add your organisation's own domain. See [Domains and corporate SSO](/en/ailita/admin/dominios-y-sso).
- **Still stuck:** [write to us](/en/ayuda/soporte).

### Another organisation already verified that domain.

- **Symptom:** when you verify a domain, you see this message.
- **Why:** another organisation already verified that domain.
- **What to do:** check that the domain is the right one. If it's your organisation's, write to us.
- **Still stuck:** [write to us](/en/ayuda/soporte).

### We can't find the TXT record yet (DNS can take a few minutes).

- **Symptom:** when you select **Verify**, you see this message.
- **Why:** the DNS doesn't show the record yet, or the record hasn't been created.
- **What to do:** check that you created the TXT record the Backoffice shows, wait a few minutes and select **Verify** again.
- **Still stuck:** [write to us](/en/ayuda/soporte).

### Verify the domain in Domains first.

- **Symptom:** when you set up SSO or the billing mailbox, you see this message.
- **Why:** the domain isn't verified yet.
- **What to do:** verify it in **Domains** and come back. See [Domains and corporate SSO](/en/ailita/admin/dominios-y-sso).
- **Still stuck:** [write to us](/en/ayuda/soporte).

### Link your Microsoft organisation first.

- **Symptom:** when you set up SSO with Microsoft, you see this message.
- **Why:** your Microsoft organisation isn't linked yet.
- **What to do:** select **Link Microsoft organisation** and set up SSO again.
- **Still stuck:** [write to us](/en/ayuda/soporte).

## Billing mailbox

### You signed in with another account. You must sign in with the mailbox's account.

- **Symptom:** when you connect the mailbox, you see this message.
- **Why:** in Microsoft you signed in with an account that isn't the mailbox's.
- **What to do:** select **Connect with Microsoft** again and sign in with the mailbox's account, not your own.
- **Still stuck:** [write to us](/en/ayuda/soporte).

### That mailbox belongs to another Microsoft organisation.

- **Symptom:** when you connect the mailbox, you see this message.
- **Why:** the mailbox belongs to a Microsoft organisation other than the one you linked.
- **What to do:** use a mailbox from your Microsoft organisation.
- **Still stuck:** [write to us](/en/ayuda/soporte).

### Microsoft didn't grant permanent access. Accept every permission.

- **Symptom:** when you connect the mailbox, you see this message.
- **Why:** you didn't accept all the permissions Microsoft asked for.
- **What to do:** connect the mailbox again and accept every permission.
- **Still stuck:** [write to us](/en/ayuda/soporte).

### You cancelled the connection at Microsoft.

- **Symptom:** when you connect the mailbox, you see this message.
- **Why:** you cancelled the process at Microsoft.
- **What to do:** select **Connect with Microsoft** again and complete the steps.
- **Still stuck:** [write to us](/en/ayuda/soporte).

### The connection took too long. Please try again.

- **Symptom:** when you connect the mailbox, you see this message.
- **Why:** the process was left open too long.
- **What to do:** try again and complete the steps without delay.
- **Still stuck:** [write to us](/en/ayuda/soporte).

### Microsoft stopped accepting the access.

- **Symptom:** the mailbox shows as **Disconnected** and you see this message.
- **Why:** Microsoft no longer accepts the access that was granted.
- **What to do:** select **Reconnect with Microsoft**, sign in with the mailbox's account and accept every permission. See [Billing mailbox](/en/ailita/admin/casilla-de-facturacion).
- **Still stuck:** [write to us](/en/ayuda/soporte).

## Access recovery

### Confirm the person's identity through another channel before approving.

- **Symptom:** when you approve a request, you see this message.
- **Why:** you didn't tick that you confirmed the person's identity.
- **What to do:** confirm their identity through another channel (call, video call or in person), tick the box and approve. See [Access recovery](/en/ailita/admin/recuperacion-de-acceso).
- **Still stuck:** [write to us](/en/ayuda/soporte).

### That request was already decided.

- **Symptom:** when you approve or reject a request, you see this message.
- **Why:** another administrator, or you, already decided on that request.
- **What to do:** reload the list and check its status.
- **Still stuck:** [write to us](/en/ayuda/soporte).

### The request expired: the person must ask again.

- **Symptom:** when you approve a request, you see this message.
- **Why:** the request expired.
- **What to do:** ask the person to send another request.
- **Still stuck:** [write to us](/en/ayuda/soporte).
