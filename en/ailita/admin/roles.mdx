A role bundles permissions. Instead of granting permissions one by one, you assign a role to each person.

## Types of role

In **Roles** you see them in three groups:

| Group | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Base** | Roles that come with Ailita. They can't be changed. |
| **Product** | Roles for each product you've contracted, such as Alfred. They can't be changed. |
| **Organisation** | The ones you create for your organisation. |

Base and product roles are read-only. You build your organisation's roles from the permissions you hold.

## Roles worth knowing

### Organisation administrator

Lets you manage your organisation in the Backoffice:

- users, invitations, sessions, devices and activity;
- roles;
- the organisation: settings, session policy, domains, corporate sign-in and the billing mailbox;
- approving or rejecting access recovery requests;
- seeing integrations and regenerating their credentials.

### Alfred operator

The **Operator** role is the one people who work with invoices in Alfred need. It allows:

- **Process documents**
- **Download PDFs and document files**
- **Read invoices**
- **Download invoice PDFs**

If someone sees the message "You don't have permission to claim tasks." in Alfred, they're missing this role. Assign it from their page in [Users](/en/ailita/admin/usuarios-e-invitaciones).

## Create a role

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the form">
    In **Roles**, select **New role**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a name and description">
    Choose a name that isn't in use. If one like it already exists, the Backoffice responds "A role with that name already exists."
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the permissions">
    Tick the permissions you want to include.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Save the role. You can now assign it to the people in your organisation.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Rules for granting permissions

- **You can only grant permissions you hold yourself.** If you try to grant one you don't have, the Backoffice responds "You can only grant permissions you hold yourself…".
- **Someone must remain to manage roles.** The organisation needs at least one active person with the permission to manage roles. If a change would leave it with nobody, the Backoffice doesn't allow it.

## Assign roles to a person

When you [invite a person](/en/ailita/admin/usuarios-e-invitaciones) you choose their role. Later, you can change roles from their page in **Users**.
