With corporate SSO, your people sign in with their Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace work account. To turn it on, you first verify that your organisation's domain is yours. A verified domain also enables the [billing mailbox](/en/ailita/admin/casilla-de-facturacion).

## Verify your domain

You need to be able to create records in your domain's DNS, or ask whoever manages it to do it.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add the domain">
    In **Domains**, add your organisation's domain, for example the one your work emails use.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create the TXT record">
    The Backoffice shows you a TXT record. Create it in your domain's DNS.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify it">
    Select **Verify**. If the DNS hasn't updated yet, the Backoffice responds "We can't find the TXT record yet (DNS can take a few minutes)." Wait a while and select **Verify** again.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Some domains can't be added:

- Public mail domains, such as gmail.com or outlook.com, can't belong to an organisation.
- A domain that another organisation already verified can't be verified again.

## Set up corporate SSO

In **Corporate SSO** you choose the provider: **Microsoft 365** or **Google Workspace**. Then you choose which application to use for sign-in:

- **Nesolva's app**: you have nothing to set up.
- **Own app**: you use an application your organisation registered with the provider. You enter its client ID and its secret.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Verify the domain">
    The email domain has to be verified. If it isn't, the Backoffice responds "Verify the domain in Domains first."
  </Step>
  <Step title="Link your Microsoft organisation (Microsoft only)">
    If you use Microsoft, select **Link Microsoft organisation**. Without this step, the Backoffice responds "Link your Microsoft organisation first."
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete the setup">
    Choose the email domain. Decide whether to create users automatically when they sign in with SSO: if you don't turn it on, people need an invitation to enter with SSO.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn it on">
    Enable SSO and save.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What your people see

When you turn on SSO, the sign-in screen shows **Continuar con Google** or **Continuar con Microsoft** (Continue with Google, Continue with Microsoft). If in [Access and security](/en/ailita/admin/acceso-y-seguridad) you chose **Corporate SSO only**, people go straight to the provider, without going through a password.

<Tip>
  Before switching to SSO only, check that you can sign in with SSO using a test account, so you don't leave your people without access.
</Tip>

If someone has trouble signing in with SSO, see [I can't sign in](/en/ailita/problemas/iniciar-sesion).
