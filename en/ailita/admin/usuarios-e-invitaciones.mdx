In **Users** you see the people in your organisation. You can search for them, invite others and manage each account.

## Find people

Search by exact email. The list shows the person, their status, their roles, whether they have a second factor and their last sign-in.

| Status | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Active** | The account works normally. |
| **Not verified** | The person hasn't confirmed their email yet. |
| **Disabled** | Someone in your organisation disabled the account. |
| **Locked** | The person's sign-in is locked. If you need help with a locked account, write to us. |

## Invite a person

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the form">
    In **Users**, select **Invite user**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the details">
    Type the person's **Email** and choose their **Rol** (role). The role comes preselected as USER, and you can change it. To see what each role allows, see [Roles](/en/ailita/admin/roles).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send the invitation">
    Select **Send invitation**. The person gets an email with a link to create their account. The link expires after a few days.
  </Step>
</Steps>

In **Invitations** you see the pending invitations, with the email, when it was sent and when it expires. From that list you can cancel an invitation.

If the person already has an account, the Backoffice responds "That person already has an account." In that case you don't need to invite them: find them in the list and review their roles.

<Note>
  If your organisation lets anyone create an account, you don't need to invite anyone. You choose that option in [Access and security](/en/ailita/admin/acceso-y-seguridad).
</Note>

## View and manage an account

When you open a person you see their status, whether their email is verified, their second factor, when the account was created and their last sign-in. You also see their devices and recent activity.

From that screen you can:

- **Disable** the account. Their sessions are closed and the person can't sign in.
- **Reactivate** a disabled account.
- Change their roles.

<Warning>
  You can't disable or delete your own account. The Backoffice responds "You can't do that to your own account." Ask another administrator to do it.
</Warning>

### Account security

The **Security** card is for when someone loses their phone or you suspect unauthorised access to their account. It has three actions:

- **Close all sessions**: the person has to sign in again on all their devices.
- **Reset second factor**: their authenticator app and passkeys are removed, and the person sets them up again at their next sign-in. Before you do it, confirm their identity through another channel, as in [Access recovery](/en/ailita/admin/recuperacion-de-acceso).
- **Delete user**: deletes the account and can't be undone. Only an administrator of the organisation can delete a user: people can't delete their own account.

If the Backoffice asks you to confirm your identity before one of these actions, follow the steps explained in [The Backoffice](/en/ailita/admin/introduccion) and repeat the action.
