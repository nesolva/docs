When you open one of your organisation's applications, such as Alfred, you land on the Ailita sign-in screen. At the top you see your organisation's logo, name and colour, and the note **Continue to** followed by the application's name.

<Note>
The steps you see depend on your organisation: password, email code, passkey, Google or Microsoft, and whether a second factor is required. If a step on this page doesn't appear, your organisation doesn't use it.
</Note>

## Sign in step by step

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enter your email">
    On **Good to see you.**, type your **Email** and select **Continue**.

    - If you prefer a passkey, select **Sign in with a passkey** and use your device's face, finger or PIN.
    - If your organisation uses Google or Microsoft, select **Continuar con Google** or **Continuar con Microsoft** (Continue with Google, Continue with Microsoft).
    - If your organisation allows sign-up, you also see Don't have an account? with the **Create account** link. See [Create your account](/en/ailita/crear-cuenta).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your organisation (if asked)">
    If your email belongs to several organisations, **Choose your organisation** appears. Choose the one you want to enter.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your password or use another option">
    On **Your password**, type your password. From this screen you can also:

    - select **Forgot your password?** to recover it. See [Forgot your password](/en/ailita/recuperar/contrasena);
    - select **Email me a code instead**, if your organisation allows it;
    - select **Use another email** to go back to the previous step.

    If your organisation uses corporate SSO, you see your provider's button instead of the password. If SSO is the only way to sign in, you go straight to the provider.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the code we emailed you (if asked)">
    You'll see one of two screens:

    - **Check your email**: we sent you a code.
    - **Confirm it's you**: we sent you a code because it's the first time you sign in from this device.

    Type the whole code and it's sent on its own. If it didn't arrive, select **Resend code**. You'll see **We sent you a new code.**
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your second factor (if you have one)">
    On **Two-step verification**, type the code from your authenticator app or one of your backup codes.

    Lost your phone and your codes? Select **Lost your phone and backup codes?** and follow [I lost my second factor](/en/ailita/recuperar/segundo-factor).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Protect your account (if your organisation requires it)">
    If your organisation requires a second factor and you don't have one yet, **Protect your account** appears.

    1. Scan the QR code with Google Authenticator, 1Password, Authy or a similar app. If you can't scan it, select **Can't scan? Enter this key:** and type the key into your app.
    2. Type the code the app shows.
    3. On **Save your backup codes**, select **Copy** and keep them somewhere safe. Each code works once, and you only see them this time. Then select **I saved them**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a passkey (optional)">
    On **Sign in faster next time**, select **Create a passkey** to sign in without typing your password. If you'd rather not, select **Not now**. You can add a passkey later from [Security](/en/ailita/cuenta/seguridad).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Decide what you share with the application (the first time)">
    The first time an application asks for your information, **You choose what to share.** appears with the list of what the application will be able to do:

    - recognise you when you come back;
    - see your first and last name;
    - see your email address;
    - see the organisation you belong to;
    - see your roles in the organisation;
    - keep you signed in while you use it.

    Select **Allow** to continue or **Cancel** to give no access. You can remove access later in [Connected apps](/en/ailita/cuenta/aplicaciones).
  </Step>
</Steps>

When you finish, Ailita takes you back to the application. In the meantime you see **Checking your session…** and a note that we're taking you back to the application.

## Confirm it's you

Before a sensitive change, an application or your own account may ask you to confirm it. **Confirm it's you** appears and you can confirm with:

- a code from your authenticator app;
- a code we email you;
- **Confirm with a passkey**.

After you confirm, you return to the same page and repeat the change. If you don't have a second factor, you sign in again.

## Sign out

To sign out, choose **Cerrar sesión** (sign out) in **My account**. You'll see **You signed out**. To come back, select **Sign in again**.

## If the application doesn't let you continue

When an application sends you to sign in and something fails, **We can't continue** appears. Go back to the application and start again. If it keeps happening, contact that application's support.

## If you can't sign in

- See [I can't sign in](/en/ailita/problemas/iniciar-sesion) for the error messages.
- See [Codes and second factor](/en/ailita/problemas/codigos-y-segundo-factor) if the code doesn't arrive or doesn't work.
